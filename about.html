Who We Are

E.CO.S.M Ltd (Entreprise de Construction, Soudure et Mobilier) is a Rwandan construction company dedicated to delivering high-quality infrastructure and interior solutions across the country.

Founded with a vision to transform the built environment in Rwanda, we combine technical expertise with a deep commitment to quality, safety, and client satisfaction on every project.

From residential homes and commercial buildings to road construction, precision welding, and custom furnishings — we are your one-stop partner for all construction needs.
