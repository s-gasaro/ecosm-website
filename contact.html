Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about working with E.CO.S.M.

How do I get a cost estimate?

Simply fill in the contact form above or call us directly. We offer free initial consultations and cost estimates for all project types.

Do you work outside of Kigali?

Yes — we take on projects across Rwanda, including rural areas. Travel and logistics costs may apply depending on location.

How long does a typical project take?

Timelines vary by project type and size. A residential home typically takes 6–12 months, while smaller jobs can be completed in weeks.

Do you provide a project warranty?

Yes — all our construction and furnishing work comes with a warranty. Details are provided in your project contract before work begins.
